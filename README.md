## What cash back won't cover (read this before a big order)

This is the information that separates a successful cash back claim from a frustrating one, and it comes from the portals' own exclusion terms:

- **Excluded products:** lumber, composite decking, and generators earn 0%. Bulk building materials like insulation and concrete are excluded too.
- **Excluded purchases:** gift cards (physical or digital), professional installation services (flooring, fencing), and business or resale purchases.
